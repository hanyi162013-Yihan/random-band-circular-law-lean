The **9/9** coverage claim concerns the nine named-result proof chains in
Section 4, not the whole paper. The precise assumptions and conclusions are
those of the listed Lean theorem statements. The proof corrections recorded
below are part of this formalization boundary.

shared Lake/mathlib project layout.

Status meanings:

- **proved**: checked Lean theorem, with no `sorry` or custom axiom;
- **certificate theorem**: conclusion is proved from explicit intermediate
  matrix equalities or operator certificates;
- **interface theorem**: the conclusion is proved from explicitly stated
  paper hypotheses or reusable intermediate inputs.

## Three source corrections forced by the formalization

1. In the companion formula, `c_i^T s_i` is a bilinear dot product.  It is
   not the Hermitian inner product over `ℂ`; the Lean definitions therefore
   introduce no complex conjugation.
2. In the singleton-word suffix proof, a canonical particle starts at
   `2W-r+k`, not `2W+k`.  `ResetWord.correctedPrefixPosition_lt` checks the
   corrected index is in range.
3. After conditioning on the complete orthogonal vector `(V_j)`, the
   directional coordinates `(U_j)` are independent but generally not
   identically distributed: coordinate `j` has law `Law(U | V = V_j)`.
   The corrected formal interface is therefore the heterogeneous
   `directionalProductKernel`; the older IID-kernel statement is valid only
   when the one-coordinate conditional law is constant in `V`.

## Trust boundary

There are no local axiom declarations.  The paper-specific periodic
determinant chain is closed by `paperXSubZI_det_eq_clearedSignedCompoundTrace`.
All nine named results in Section 4 have checked main-chain declarations.
The precise scope is given by the assumptions and conclusions of those Lean
theorems; this statement does not extend to Sections 5--6 or the whole paper.
The remaining probabilistic assumptions are ordinary theorem parameters or
structure fields taken from the paper: atom normalization and independence,
bounded density or raw directional conditional density, normalized second
moments, positive profile weights, and stated nondegeneracy conditions.

The directional regular conditional product is constructed from the raw
paper hypothesis and identified with the actual rotated IID atom vector.
The literal fresh-block `L¹` closures (planar-complex, real, and directional)
derive zero-set nullity, a.e. positivity, and both logarithmic halves internally.
For a measurable past-dependent frozen family, the complex/real product-law
wrappers provide joint integrability and the canonical conditional-expectation
identity.  Projective positive-log integrability is derived from the second
moment rather than an external `hexcess`.  The final complex, real, and
directional pressure `_auto` specializations likewise derive global `MemLp 2`
and raw replacement outer Bochner integrability internally.  No local axiom
hides any of these inputs.
